PROPERTY FEATURES

>> 3,700 ft. of Riverfront
>> 6 Miles from G.P. Hwy
>> 30 mins to Capital

A 100-acre riverfront property in the More Tomorrow area of the Cayo District! This stunning parcel features over 3,700 ft. of Belize River frontage and enjoys rich, alluvial soil and relatively flat land, making it perfect for farming, evident of other large farms in the area. The property is less than 6 miles from the George Price Highway and has solid access. Being a stone’s throw away from the village also allows ease of access to hired hands in the case of wanting to do a farming operation or other development. In addition, utilities are available less than 1 mile away. The entire parcel is in a natural state, allowing new owners to do as they see fit. For other conveniences, the capital city of Belmopan is approximately 30 minutes away and provides all daily needed amenities such as banks, supermarkets, hardware stores, etc. This parcel is a brilliant investment opportunity and would make for a stunning ranch or community development. Take advantage of a great opportunity to own riverfront acreage at a competitive price per acre in the heart of Western Belize!
